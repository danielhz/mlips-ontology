\section{Sustainability and Maintenance}
\label{sec:sustainability}
%% ====================================================================

The ontology is developed within the META-LEARN project, funded by the
ERC (Advanced Grant, 5~years). The source is hosted on the University of
Stuttgart's Git infrastructure, with versioned releases deposited in
DaRUS~\cite{darus2024} with persistent DOIs. By building on OWL, SHACL, and
standard Semantic Web formats, the ontology is independent of any specific
software implementation.

We plan to engage the MLIP community through workshops and integration with
existing materials science data repositories (NOMAD, Materials Cloud), and
to contribute to Wikidata's coverage of MLIP algorithms and benchmarks.
