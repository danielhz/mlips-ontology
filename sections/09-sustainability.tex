\section{Sustainability and Maintenance}
\label{sec:sustainability}
%% ====================================================================

The resources are developed within the META-LEARN project, funded by the
ERC (Advanced Grant, 5~years). Source code is hosted on the University of
Stuttgart's Git server and mirrored on GitHub, GitLab, and Codeberg. The
\MLIPsKG{} and versioned ontology releases are deposited in
DaRUS~\cite{darus2024} with persistent DOIs. By building on RDF, OWL, SPARQL,
and SHACL, the resources are independent of any specific software
implementation.

We plan to engage the MLIP community through workshops and integration with
NOMAD and Materials Cloud, and to contribute to Wikidata's coverage of MLIP
research.
