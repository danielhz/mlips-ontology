\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ====================================================================

We have presented the \MLIPsOntology{}, an OWL\,2~DL ontology that captures
the metadata landscape of machine learning interatomic potential research.
The ontology's three modules---Algorithm, Training Data, and
Benchmark---address a gap not covered by existing ML or materials science
ontologies: the specific vocabulary needed to describe how MLIPs are trained,
evaluated, and compared. The ontology includes 27 formal axioms that enforce
data completeness and consistency, and is aligned with ML-Schema, MDO,
CMSO/ASMO, and PROV-O for interoperability.

The running example with Moment Tensor Potentials demonstrated how the
ontology captures the full lifecycle of an MLIP study---from algorithm
hyperparameters and DFT training data to trained models and benchmark
results---and how the three-level distinction between hyperparameter
definitions, settings, and learned parameters is reflected in the ontology
design. The evaluation showed that all eight competency questions are
answerable as SPARQL queries, and that the ontology is consistent under
OWL reasoning. Companion SHACL shapes enforce the axioms at the data level.

By building on open Semantic Web standards, we ensure extensibility as the
field evolves. The ontology is published under CC BY 4.0 with a persistent
IRI and is archived on DaRUS for long-term availability.
