\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ====================================================================

We have presented the \MLIPsOntology{} and the \MLIPsKG{}---an OWL ontology and
a SPARQL-queryable knowledge graph that capture the metadata landscape of
machine learning interatomic potential research. The ontology's three
modules---Algorithm, Training Data, and Benchmark---address a gap not covered
by existing ML or materials science ontologies: the specific vocabulary needed
to describe how MLIPs are trained, evaluated, and compared. SHACL shapes enforce
data quality and enable form-based data entry. The \MLIPE{} extension modules
for \Concon{} add domain-specific capabilities---custom SPARQL functions for
atomistic computations, simulation data handling, and specialized AI
extraction---while leveraging \Concon{}'s general-purpose infrastructure for
team collaboration, literature management, and SHACL-driven editing. \SemASE{}
provides a programmatic bridge from simulation toolkits to the knowledge graph.

Together, these resources enable researchers to systematically compare MLIP
studies, discover relevant training datasets and hyperparameters, and build
on prior work through structured, queryable knowledge rather than ad-hoc
literature searches. By building on open Semantic Web standards and aligning
with established materials science and ML ontologies, we ensure
interoperability and extensibility as the field evolves.
