The MTP method uses L-BFGS as its training algorithm. The training algorithm is an \texttt{mls:Algorithm}, which is the sole alignment point between MLIPs ontology and ML-Schema.
\begin{lstlisting}[language=turtle]
ex:MTP mlips:hasTrainingAlgorithm ex:lbfgs .
ex:lbfgs a mls:Algorithm ;
    rdfs:label "L-BFGS" .
\end{lstlisting}
